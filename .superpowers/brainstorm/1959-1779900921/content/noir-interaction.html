How does the Noir paginated experience work?

Each section becomes a full-screen "slide." Here are 3 ways to navigate between them.

A

Click / tap anywhere to advance

The entire screen is a tap target. Tap to go forward, tap a back arrow to go back. Minimal UI — nothing distracts from the content. Each section fills the viewport.
